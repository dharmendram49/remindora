Remindora

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Our approach

Remindora is designed to help you organize reminders for tasks, medicines, water intake, appointments, bills, and shopping. Your information is primarily stored on your device. We do not require an account to use the app.

Information stored in the app

When you use Remindora, the app stores the information you enter locally on your device. This can include:

  • task, medicine, appointment, bill, water-intake, and shopping-list details;
  • reminder schedules, notification settings, completion history, and app preferences; and
  • an optional profile image or display information that you choose to add.

This information is used to provide the features you choose, including showing your schedules and delivering reminders. Remindora does not operate a server that receives this app data as part of normal use.

Notifications and device permissions

With your permission, Remindora uses local notifications to alert you about scheduled reminders. On Android, the app may request notification, alarm, battery-optimization, and restart-related permissions so reminders can work reliably, including after a device restart. These permissions are used on your device to schedule and show reminders; they are not used to read your personal notifications.

Location and weather

Remindora may ask for your location to display your current area and local temperature in the app header. Location access is optional. If you allow it, the app sends the coordinates needed for that feature to Open-Meteo for weather information and BigDataCloud for reverse geocoding. The app does not store a location history.

Backups and Google Drive

You can choose to create a backup of your Remindora data. Backups are JSON files containing your reminder data and app settings. They are saved to device storage or shared through your device's share sheet, such as to Google Drive. When you choose Google Drive backup, Google handles the selected account and the destination you choose. Remindora does not automatically upload backups or access your Drive files.

Advertising

Remindora may display ads through Google AdMob. Where required, AdMob presents a consent flow before requesting ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and advertising interaction data to provide, measure, and personalize ads according to your choices and their policies. You can review Google's privacy information at policies.google.com/privacy.

Support

If you contact support, we receive the information you include in your email, such as your email address and the details of your request. We use it only to respond to and support your request.

Your choices and controls

  • You can deny or revoke location and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • You can delete individual items in the app or use the clear-data option to remove your locally stored Remindora data.
  • You control whether to create, share, keep, or delete backups. Deleting the app or clearing app storage may remove local data, so keep a backup if you want to retain it.
  • Advertising choices can be managed through the AdMob consent options when available and through your device's privacy and advertising settings.
Important: Medicine and health reminders are organizational tools only. They are not medical advice and should not replace professional medical guidance.

Children's privacy

Remindora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be made available with Remindora and will show its updated date.

Contact us

For privacy questions, contact us at support@remindora.app.
